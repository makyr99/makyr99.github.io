Privacy policy

Hyperfocus · last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We don't run servers, and we never receive your game data.
  • The first time you open the app, it asks your age group. The answer stays on your phone and sets up ads, statistics, purchases and reminders for your age (see "Your age").
  • Your progress, coins, purchases and settings are saved on your phone only.
  • Optional in-app purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card or other payment details.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to show ads, measure them and stop fraud.
  • If you are 16 or older, the app sends game statistics (which games and levels you play, for example) to Google Analytics, so we can see what to fix. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland only if you agree.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland you decide whether ads can be personalized and whether statistics are collected, and you can change your answer in Settings at any time.
  • A daily reminder notification is optional. It is off unless you turn it on.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Who we are

Hyperfocus is a free mini-games app made by Hyperfocus Labs ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to hyperfocusgames.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your levels, coins, unlocked games and themes, best scores, streak, missions and settings in the app's own storage on your device. None of it is sent to us. The same goes for your purchases: the app keeps a note of what you bought (VIP, the starter pack, revive tokens) and of each purchase's reference from Google Play, so that nothing is added twice.

  • To delete it, use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the app.
  • If your phone backs up apps to your Google account (Android's automatic backup), your saved progress can be included in that backup. Google stores it under your account, and we can't see it.

Your age

The first time you open Hyperfocus, it asks which age group you are in: under 13, 13 to 15, 16 or 17, or 18 and older. The answer is saved on your phone and isn't sent to us. The app uses it like this:

  • Under 13: ads are marked as directed at children and limited to content suitable for everyone (Google's "G" rating), with no personalization. There are no purchases and no shop, no game statistics and no notifications.
  • 13 to 15: ads are marked as being for someone under the age of consent (16 in Romania), so they are not personalized and the app doesn't ask for consent to personalized ads. No game statistics are collected.
  • 16 and older: in the EU, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent (see "Your choices"); game statistics are collected only if you agree.

The app asks once. Reset progress keeps the answer; uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it, and the app asks again.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Hyperfocus is free because it shows ads. Some ads are optional "watch to get a reward" ads (a revive, a hint, extra coins), and some appear between games. Ads come from Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is software from Google that runs inside the app. When it loads or shows an ad, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your exact location)
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system version and language
  • how you interact with ads and the app, such as ad views, taps and app launches
  • crash and performance information about the ad software

Google uses this to show ads, to measure how ads perform, to prevent fraud and abuse and, only if you allow it, to personalize ads. Google handles this data under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We limit ads to content suitable for teens and up (Google's "T" rating), and for players under 13 to content suitable for everyone ("G"). For players under 16 we tell Google not to personalize ads (see "Your age").

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see which games are too hard, where players stop and whether ads and prices are fair, the app sends us statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service. This happens only if you are 16 or older and, in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, only if you allowed "store and/or access information on a device" in the consent form. Players under 16 are never counted.

  • What is sent: what you do in the game, such as starting and finishing a level (which game, which level, win or fail, how many tries), finishing your first game, opening the daily chest, watching a reward ad or an ad between games, and which item you bought and its price (never your payment details). Google Analytics also records app opens and sessions, your device model, Android version, language and app version, a random ID for this app install, and a general location (country or region) that Google works out from your IP address.
  • What is not sent: your name, email, contacts, precise location or the advertising ID. The statistics are not linked to your Google account and are not used to choose the ads you see.
  • We only see totals and trends in Google Analytics, such as "how many players finish level 5". Google processes the data for us under the Firebase data processing terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Reminders and ratings

  • Daily reminder (optional). After your first daily chest the app asks whether you want a reminder. Only if you say yes does Android ask for permission to show notifications. You then get at most one notification a day, around 18:00, and only on days you haven't opened the game. If you ignore three in a row, the reminder switches itself off. It is scheduled on your phone: there is no notification server, and no data is sent for it. Turn it off any time in Settings > Daily reminder. Players under 13 never get notifications.
  • Rating the app. Once in a while, after a big win, the app may show Google Play's own "rate this app" window. Rating is optional, and the rating and any review go to Google Play under its terms. The app never learns whether or how you rated it.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can play everything for free. If you choose to buy something (coins, revive tokens, the starter pack or VIP), the purchase is made and paid for through Google Play, under Google Play's terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We never see your card, bank or other payment details. Google handles the payment.
  • The app keeps its record of your purchases on your phone (see above) and sends it nowhere. Uninstalling the app or using Reset progress deletes coins and revive tokens, including bought ones. VIP and the starter pack can be restored from Google Play with Settings > Restore purchases.
  • For accounting, tax and refunds, Google Play gives us order records (such as the order number, the item, price and date, and the buyer's country or general area). We use them only for those purposes and keep them as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play. You can ask for one in the Play Store, or write to us.

Your choices

  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, if you are 16 or older, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown and before game statistics are collected. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad choices. If you say no, you still get ads, but they won't be personalized, and no statistics are sent. Taking back your consent also deletes the statistics ID and any unsent statistics on your phone.
  • Daily reminder: off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off in Settings > Daily reminder or in your phone's notification settings.
  • On any Android phone, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Permissions

The app asks Android for internet and network access (to load ads and send game statistics), vibration (for haptic feedback), the advertising ID and Android's ad services (added by Google's ad software), Google Play billing (added by Google's billing software, so you can make purchases) and Google Play's install referrer (added by Google Analytics, to tell which store page an install came from). For the optional daily reminder it asks for notifications (Android 13 and later ask you first, and only after you said yes in the game) and to run after the phone restarts, so the reminder keeps its schedule. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Children

Hyperfocus is made for players aged 14 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If a player says they are under 13, the app shows only child-directed, non-personalized ads rated for everyone, has no shop or purchases, sends no game statistics and shows no notifications. Players under 16 (the age of digital consent in Romania) get non-personalized ads and are never counted in game statistics, and the app doesn't ask them for consent.

Purchases cost real money. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you buy anything. Parents can make Google Play ask for approval or a password before every purchase.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share any data with anyone ourselves. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's ad software collects, the game statistics (players 16 and older, with consent where required), both as described above, and what Google Play handles when you choose to buy something.

Security and how long data is kept

Apart from Google Play's order records for purchases (see above) and the game statistics, we don't keep any of your data, because we never receive it. Game statistics are deleted from Google Analytics after 14 months at the latest. Google's software sends its data over encrypted connections, and Google decides how long to keep ad data under its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Apart from order records for purchases and the game statistics, we don't hold personal data about you, but you can still write to us at the address above. Game statistics are tied only to a random ID for your app install, not to your name, so we can't look them up for you. Taking back your consent in Settings > Privacy & ad choices (in the EU, the UK and Switzerland) or uninstalling the app removes that ID from your phone, so nothing new is linked to what was sent before, and what was sent is deleted after 14 months at the latest. For data collected through ads, you can also use Google's controls at My Ad Center.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you inside the app.
